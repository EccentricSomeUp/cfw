## New findings this pass

| ID | Severity | Location | Summary | Disposition |
|----|----------|----------|---------|-------------|
| H-001 | High | `src/cfw/dispatch.c` `cfw_dispatch_init` | `max_middleware * sizeof(cfw_dispatch_middleware_t)` could overflow `size_t`, producing an undersized arena allocation and heap overflow when middleware is registered | Fixed: reject `max_middleware > SIZE_MAX / sizeof(...)` |
| H-002 | High | `src/cfw/server.c` `cfw_server_init`/accept | `max_headers`/`max_params` products were unchecked; a huge configured value could wrap the malloc size and overflow the scratch arrays during dispatch | Fixed: init validates both against `SIZE_MAX / sizeof(entry)` before any allocation |
| H-003 | Info | `include/cfw/*.h` | Public structs expose fields directly (project convention); mutation between init and use is caller responsibility | Accepted: consistent with existing types; documented per-type |
